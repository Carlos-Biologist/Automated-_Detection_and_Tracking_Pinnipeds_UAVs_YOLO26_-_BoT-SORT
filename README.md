# Automated-_Detection_and_Tracking_Pinnipeds_UAVs_YOLO26_-_BoT-SORT
Computational pipelines for the detection, taxonomic classification, and spatio-temporal tracking of South American pinnipeds using UAV imagery and video across two coastal marine protected areas in southern Brazil: the Wildlife Refuge of Ilha dos Lobos (WRIL) and the Wildlife Refuge Molhe Leste (WRML).
